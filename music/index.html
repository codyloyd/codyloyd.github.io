Music

I enjoy writing and performing music. In a past life, I was a high-school band teacher, and I regularly play guitar in my church's band. I'm currently learning Banjo! Here you can find some of the music I've written over the years.

melos

I built The Melos Project — a free, open music education suite. It has a structured theory curriculum and a bunch of practice tools for drilling note names, chords, intervals, scales, and key signatures. And it's FREE!

the decreasing

At this point, this is my main musical creative project, along with my brother-in-law Micah. We've recorded several songs together over the last few years and have released a couple of albums. You can find links to all of the streaming platforms on our website. https://thedecreasing.com/

film music

There is no film associated with this music of course, but I enjoy composing and producing this kind of atmospheric orchestral music.
