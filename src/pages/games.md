The trick is to learn the movement patterns of the enemies. Play it [HERE](https://codyloyd.itch.io/roosta)!

## dungeon song

![Dungeon Song](/images/dungeon-song.png)

I am very proud of the concept for this game. It's a musical puzzler where you have to listen and decipher the melody that's being played to unlock the door.
It takes a little practice to get a feel for where the notes live.. so give it a little time. Play it [HERE](https://codyloyd.itch.io/dungeon-song)
